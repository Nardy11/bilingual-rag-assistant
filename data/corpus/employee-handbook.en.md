# Falak Home Systems — Employee Handbook (2026 edition)

> Falak Home Systems is a fictional company invented for this demo. Any resemblance to a real company is accidental. This text is released under CC BY 4.0.

## 1. Working hours

The standard working week runs from Sunday to Thursday. Office hours are 8:00 to 16:00, with a one-hour lunch break, for a total of 35 working hours per week. During the holy month of Ramadan, working hours are reduced to six hours per day, from 9:00 to 15:00. Employees who work on a public holiday at their manager's request receive a replacement day off, to be taken within 60 days.

## 2. Annual leave

Every full-time employee is entitled to 30 calendar days of paid annual leave per year. Leave accrues at a rate of 2.5 days for each month of service. Employees may carry over a maximum of 10 unused days into the next calendar year; any balance above 10 days is forfeited on 31 March. Leave requests must be submitted in the HR portal at least 14 days before the first day of leave. Requests of three days or fewer need only 48 hours' notice.

## 3. Sick leave

Employees receive 15 days of sick leave per year at full pay, followed by 10 days at half pay. A medical certificate from a licensed clinic is required for any absence longer than two consecutive days. Sick leave cannot be carried over to the next year.

## 4. Family and special leave

Female employees are entitled to 70 days of maternity leave at full pay. Fathers receive 5 working days of paternity leave, to be taken within the first month after the birth. In the event of the death of a close relative, employees may take 3 days of bereavement leave. Muslim employees may take 21 days of paid Hajj leave once during their employment, after completing two years of service.

## 5. Probation and notice

New employees serve a probation period of 100 days. During probation, either party may end the contract with seven days' notice. After probation, the notice period is 30 days for staff and 60 days for managers and above. Employees who resign must return their laptop, badge and any company equipment on their last working day.

## 6. Remote work

After passing probation, employees may work remotely up to two days per week, with the written approval of their line manager. Remote work is not available to warehouse, installation and showroom staff, whose roles require them to be on site. When working remotely, employees must be reachable during core hours, from 10:00 to 14:00, and must connect through the company VPN.

## 7. Expenses

Business expenses are reimbursed when they are submitted through the expense app within 30 days of being incurred. A receipt is required for any single expense above 5 KWD. Approved expenses are paid with the next monthly salary. Alcohol, traffic fines and personal entertainment are never reimbursed.

## 8. Training and development

Each employee has an annual training budget of 400 KWD, which can be used for courses, certifications and conference tickets related to their role. Training requests must be approved by the line manager and by HR. An employee who leaves the company within 12 months of a company-paid certification must repay 50 percent of its cost.

## 9. Performance reviews

Performance is reviewed twice a year, in June and in December. Salary increases and bonuses are decided once a year, after the December review, and take effect from 1 January.
