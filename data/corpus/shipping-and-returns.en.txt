FALAK HOME SYSTEMS - SHIPPING, DELIVERY AND RETURNS POLICY

Falak Home Systems is a fictional company invented for this demo. Any resemblance to a real company is accidental. This text is released under CC BY 4.0.

DELIVERY AREAS AND TIMES
We deliver to all areas of Kuwait. Orders placed before 2 PM are delivered within one to two working days. Orders to other GCC countries (Saudi Arabia, the UAE, Bahrain, Qatar and Oman) are delivered within five to seven working days through our courier partner. We do not currently ship outside the GCC.

SHIPPING FEES
Delivery inside Kuwait is free for orders of 20 KWD or more; smaller orders pay a flat fee of 1.5 KWD. Shipping to other GCC countries costs 6 KWD per order, and customs duties in the destination country are paid by the customer on delivery.

TRACKING YOUR ORDER
You receive a tracking link by SMS and email as soon as your order leaves our warehouse. If the courier cannot reach you, they will try again the next working day; after two failed attempts the order is returned to the warehouse and you can collect it from our Shuwaikh showroom within 10 days.

DAMAGED OR WRONG ITEMS
If your order arrives damaged or you receive the wrong item, report it within 48 hours of delivery through the app or the website, with photos of the item and the packaging. We will collect the item and send a replacement at no cost to you.

RETURNS
Unused products in their original packaging can be returned within 14 days of delivery. Installed water filters and opened filter cartridges cannot be returned for hygiene reasons, unless they are faulty. To start a return, open the order in the app and choose "Return item"; a courier will collect it within three working days. Return collection is free inside Kuwait.

REFUNDS
Refunds are issued to the original payment method within 7 working days after we receive and inspect the returned item. KNET refunds may take an additional two to three days to appear, depending on your bank. Orders paid in cash on delivery are refunded by bank transfer.

GIFT CARDS
Falak gift cards are valid for one year from the date of purchase and can be used for any product or for the Falak Care filter subscription. Gift cards cannot be exchanged for cash.
